# INTRODUCTION

What is AIF and how does it help you.

The ideal outcome of this lesson is to set the foundation.

# CORE OUTCOMES

The main outcomes of the program are:

To understand business fundamentals before learning how to use AI to apply them. You don't make money because you're "good at AI", you make money because AI helps you do certain things that make money.

To learn how to identify real problems and create AI-powered solutions.

Build and launch offers based on those solutions, whether digital products, SaaS tools, or service packages.

Understand and apply distribution methods using social media and other channels.

Learn how AI systems actually function so you can use them efficiently and avoid common pitfalls.

# COMMUNITY & LEARNING

Join a community that shares insights, methods, and results in real-world business applications of AI.

# THE APPROACH

AIF focuses on giving you the minimum information needed to operate effectively.

> The lessons are structured to move you toward one of three outcomes: creating something new, improving an existing system, or distributing more effectively.
