# CORE DESIRES

Understanding Problems, Desires, Solutions.

> Learning Outcome: Understand what drives all purchasing decisions and how to identify valuable problems to solve.

The foundation of all business: People buy to get what they want. Not what you want. What THEY want. If you don't understand what they want, you can't sell them anything.

What do people want? Everything boils down to a few core desires:

- Make money - Get richer, increase income, grow wealth
- Save money - Keep more of what they have, reduce costs
- Save time - Get more done, automate, eliminate waste
- Status/attraction - Be respected, be attractive, gain influence

Every purchase traces back to one of these.

# UNDERSTANDING PROBLEMS

The bridge metaphor: Think of a sketchy rope bridge across a canyon. $100 on the other side = probably not worth crossing. $1,000,000 on the other side = you'd cross it. The bridge (your solution) is the same. The value changed based on what's on the other end (the desire).

Your job as a business owner: Understand what your audience desires, then build bridges to those desires. This is universal business knowledge. AI just helps you build the bridges faster.

What is a problem? A problem is anything between someone and their desire. That's it.

Example: They want to make $10k/month (desire). They don't know how to get customers (problem). Your offer teaches them to get customers (solution/bridge).

Surface problems vs root problems:

Most people solve surface problems. The real opportunity is in root problems.

Surface: "I need more traffic to my website"

Root: "I don't know how to position my offer so people want to click"

> Solving surface problems = competing with everyone. Solving root problems = less competition, more value.

# SOLUTION FORMATS

Solution formats: The same solution can be packaged different ways:

- Course teaching Facebook ads (info)
- Done-for-you ad management (service)
- Software that automates ad creation (tool)

Same problem, different bridges. All can work.

# VALUE CREATION

Solution sequences - the backend:

Solve problem #1 = first product. They hit problem #2 = second product (upsell). You're building a "problem ladder" they climb. Each rung is a product.
