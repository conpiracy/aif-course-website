# THE VALUE GAP

Identifying Why People Will Buy Your Solution.

What this lesson is: Understanding the value gap between current problem and desired future that creates willingness to pay, then identifying any edges you have.

## Current State Mapping

Definition: Where they are now with this problem - the daily pain.

Components: Money lost, time wasted, opportunities missed, emotional cost.

Key Exercise: Calculate the real cost - get specific numbers.

Example: Freelancer at $2k/month wanting $15k/month = $13k gap.

## Desired State Mapping

Definition: Where they want to be - the transformation.

Components: Revenue gained, time saved, status achieved, freedom unlocked.

Example: $15k/month difference = $180k/year value created.

## Value Calculation

> If you create $100k value, charging $10k = 10x ROI for them.

# YOUR EDGES

What Advantages You Have:

- Knowledge: What you know others don't
- Experience: What you've done others haven't
- Access: What you can reach others can't
- Technical: What you can build others can't
- Trust: Why people believe you
- Process: Systems/methods you've developed

# COMPOUNDING ADVANTAGE

> Edges compound - you want to preferably be using as many of your edges at all times.
