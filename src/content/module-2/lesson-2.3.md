# THE WRONG APPROACH

Validating Market Desire & Size - Using AI for Real Market Research.

> Learning Outcome: Validate that your problem represents a real, profitable market by researching desire depth and market size using AI tools.

The Traditional Approach (What Most People Get Wrong):

Most people think validation means: "Search Reddit for complaints." That's tactical, not strategic.

# REAL VALIDATION

Real validation answers:

- Desire depth: Is this a burning problem that connects to money/time/status?
- Market size: How much is spent solving this problem annually?
- Competition health: Are competitors making money (proof of market) or struggling?
- Your advantage: Can you differentiate on approach, positioning, or solution format?

Example of real validation:

Problem: "Neck and knee pain."

Do you see people complaining on Twitter? Not really.

Do people pay $50K+ for surgeries and physical therapy? Absolutely.

Market size: $100B+ healthcare industry.

> This is a massive, proven market—whether or not you see "complaints."

# AI ADVANTAGE

The AI Advantage in Market Research:

- Chain AI tools to gather data at scale (competitor analysis, pricing research, customer review mining)
- Use context profiles to control analysis quality (your market expertise + AI speed)
- Synthesize patterns humans would miss (analyze 10,000 reviews in 10 minutes)

> If you're bad at market research, AI just helps you be bad faster. You need what to look for first.
