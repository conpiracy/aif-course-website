# YOUR ACHIEVEMENTS

Finding Problems from Your Own Experience.

> Learning Outcome: Identify 3-5 problems you've personally solved that could become offers.

Why this works:

- You understand the pain (you felt it)
- You know the solution works (you did it)
- You have proof (your results)
- You can explain it authentically (it's your story)

The alternative: Solve problems you think exist, build something nobody wants, waste 3 months, give up.

## Step 1: What have you achieved? (10 minutes)

Think about times you: Made money doing something specific. Saved significant time with a system you built. Built something from scratch that worked. Overcame a major obstacle others struggle with.

Write down 5-10 achievements. Don't filter yet.

# FINDING PROBLEMS

## Step 2: What problems did you overcome? (10 minutes)

For each achievement, ask:
- What blocked me initially?
- What did I struggle with for months?
- What breakthrough changed everything?
- What do others still struggle with that I figured out?

# VALIDATION FILTER

## Step 3: Quick filter - Does it connect to money/time/status? (5 minutes)

Delete anything that doesn't clearly connect to money, time, or status.

## Step 4: The hidden problem check (5 minutes)

Is this what people think their problem is? (surface problem) Or is this the REAL problem underneath? (root problem)

> Root problems = less competition, more value.

Deliverable: A list of 3-5 problems you've solved that connect clearly to money/time/status.
